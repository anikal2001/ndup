The generator is `src/ndup/golden.py`: about 500 documents with planted near-copies, duplicates, subsets, partials, short copies, template rows, density rows, Spanish, Chinese, and stubs. Labels are pairs, not a canonical winner.

Thresholds for `fake-bow-v1` shipped in `analysis/thresholds.py` are τ_chunk 0.70, τ_doc 0.66, short-doc delta 0.05. `BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5` keeps 0.82 / 0.78 until `ndup calibrate` sees real labels. The quality test reports per-relation F1 and fails if duplicate+near_copy F1 drops under 0.80 or any tracked relation drops more than 0.03 from `tests/golden/baseline.json`.
