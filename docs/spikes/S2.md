# S2 — Search coverage

The live miss rate and indexing lag were not measured.

Search is a hint. The client stops a descending search at the stored watermark minus 15 minutes. Block-tree walk is the ground truth and enqueues children search did not return. A 404 parent makes the page a root. An excluded ancestor is stored with a null title and is not block-fetched.

Fixture coverage: `tests/unit/test_discovery_queue.py` omits 1 of 10 children from search and asserts the walk records it; a 404 ancestor becomes a root; an excluded page is never fetched.
